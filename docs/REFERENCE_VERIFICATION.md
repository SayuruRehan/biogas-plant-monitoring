## Edition and claim boundaries

- Reference [2] cites the US fourth edition. The Global edition has different
  chapter numbering. InformIT lists publication on 28 April 2020 and copyright
  2021; the report uses the requested 2021 edition/copyright year, not an
  invented publication date. No book DOI is claimed.
- Chen et al. supports the discussion of inhibition, including ammonia and its
  release from protein degradation. Schmidt et al. directly addresses Australian
  red-meat processing wastewater, variable organic/fat loading, pH and volatile
  fatty acids, and field sensor fouling/calibration problems (Section 3.3).
- The Stable/Stressed/Critical biological descriptions are explicitly the
  student's illustrative interpretation. Neither paper validates the supplied
  probabilities, the state boundaries, or the selected interventions.
- The correlated-error example and omitted loading-response delay are critiques
  of the assignment's modelling assumptions, not claims that these faults were
  observed in its unlabelled scenario datasets.
- The report cites the actual textbook and its decision-theory chapter in place
  of the previous online algorithm supplement. The two new peer-reviewed domain
  references appear as [5] and [6]; existing reference numbers are retained.
